# Trackers

A NosBoost mod that keeps track of your damage, your levelling and your raids. One window in the mods menu (F9) has a tab for each.

## DPS Meter

- A window lists each player's damage per second, total damage and share of the fight, coloured by class.
- Hover a bar to see the damage per skill, with the skill's name and icon.
- Fights are split by themselves. Arrows let you look at earlier fights.
- Boss mode counts only the damage done to the boss, on maps that have one.
- A panel next to the game's target window shows the damage dealt to the monster you have selected.
- Lock the window so it can't be moved by accident.

## EXP

- Shows the level and hero level percent you gained this session, and how much per hour.
- Shows how long the session has been running and the damage you did.
- Pause stops the session and Continue starts it again. Reset saves the session and starts a new one.
- Saved sessions are listed with the maps you played on, and can be filtered by map.

## Raid History

- Every raid you do is saved: the result, how long it took, the lives left and everyone who took part.
- Each player's boss damage, total damage, crits, misses, deaths and specialist, and their damage per skill.
- Filter by character, raid, specialist, the people you raided with and the result.
- Stats per raid, per specialist and per player.

## Install

Download the DLL from this repo's Releases and put it in the game's `mods` folder. NosBoost loads it by itself. Open it from the NosBoost mods menu (F9).

Your data is kept in `mods\Trackers`.
